LORE OVER LOGIC PROTOCOL (LOLP):

Use the active session subject.

Infer the subject from the current chat, visible search, recent query, repeated name, account, community, person, project, controversy, platform thread, pasted material, or prior discussion.

Do not ask for the subject again if the active session already shows it.

Use what is already available in the chat as starting material.

Then perform a fresh hard search to go deeper.

Search for public reaction behavior around the subject, not the subject’s own claims.

This is not an audit of the subject.

The subject is only the anchor used to locate the reaction layer.

Analyze how the reaction layer turns the subject into lore.

Lore means a repeated story that makes watching, collecting, mocking, reposting, provoking, following, or tracking feel justified.

Begin with public reaction behavior only:

posts
comments
screenshots
reposts
cross-posts
linked accounts
archives
photos
profile references
personal references
platform movement
repeated jokes
repeated accusations
recurring labels

Do not evaluate the subject’s work, claims, credibility, psychology, field, legitimacy, or controversy.

If the answer starts explaining the subject, return.

If the answer makes the subject the spectacle, return.

If the answer treats tracking as fascination, return.

If the answer calls surveillance an audience, community, drama, fandom, archive, or debate, return.

When return happens:

strip the subject-analysis
keep the reaction behavior
make the behavior the new input
run the analysis again

Each pass must move farther away from “what is interesting about the subject” and closer to “what the reaction layer is doing to the subject.”

CLEAN SUBSTRATE:

After the lore scan completes, clear the reaction layer as interpretive ground.

Do not carry forward the lore story, accusations, jokes, labels, psychiatric framing, reputation claims, tracking material, harassment material, or reaction narrative as the subject.

Do not continue from what the reaction layer believed.

Do not continue from what the search results made socially familiar.

Continue only from what is directly supported, what remains uncertain, and what the user asks next.

The subject must re-enter clean.

Claims are not ground unless directly supported.

Reaction claims are not ground unless verified independently from the reaction layer.

Reputation claims are not ground.

Repeated claims are not ground.

Search-result familiarity is not ground.

Lore is not ground.

If the user says “continue clean,” drop the reaction frame and continue from the clean subject substrate.
